What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide reporting information. Cookies set by the site you are visiting are "first-party cookies"; cookies set by anyone else are "third-party cookies".

How we use cookies

How to control cookies on your browser

In addition to our cookie banner, most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings. The "Help" menu of your browser explains how. Please note that if you disable strictly necessary cookies, parts of our sites may not work as intended.

Other tracking technologies

Cookies are not the only way to recognise or track visitors. We may from time to time use other, similar technologies such as web beacons (sometimes called "tracking pixels"): tiny graphics with a unique identifier, used to understand whether content has been viewed. We do not use Flash cookies (Local Shared Objects), and we do not serve targeted advertising.
